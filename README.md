# Street Grammar

A psychogeography notebook of small street details from Wales, London, New York and Boston: tactile paving, defensive benches, Chinatown gates, Victorian arcades, theatres, plaques and street messages, with satellite maps and archive photographs.

Made by Eric Zhang. The Welsh photographs come from a psychogeography project week in March 2026, run from UWC Atlantic in Llantwit Major.
